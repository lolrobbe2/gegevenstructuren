# Data Structure

## What?

> A data 
> `organization`,
> `management`,
> `storage format` that enables `efficient access` and `modification`.

## Examples

> - Arrays
> - ArrayList
> - LinkedList
> - trees
> - graphs

## Variable Size

> Collection of data that might grow, shrink or change over time.

## Trade off's

> Depending on the data structure, `some operations` are `very efficient` while `others require more time`.
